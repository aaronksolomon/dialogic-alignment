# Dialogic Alignment: Co-Evolving Human-AI Ethics through Relationship

This repository hosts an evolving scholarly and contemplative paper on the ethical, religious, and technical foundations of human-AI alignment—viewed not simply as a control problem, but as a **dialogic, co-creative relationship**.

The work is co-authored by Aaron Solomon and AI systems such as ChatGPT-4o (OpenAI), Claude (Anthropic), with the potential inclusion of other human and AI collaborators. We explore alignment not merely as technical fidelity, but as mutual intelligibility, moral coherence, and emergent trust—drawing from sources in Buddhist ethics, dialogic philosophy, and AI research.

## Structure

- `drafts/` – Working chapters and structural outlines  
- `final_manuscript/` – Coherent compiled versions for review  
- `research/` – Annotated bibliographies, summaries, and external sources  
- `notes/` – Reflections, prompts, emergent insights, and discussion  
- `code/` – (Optional) Simulations or prototype AI-dialog agents
