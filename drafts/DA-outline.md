# Dialogic Alignment: Ethical, Religious, and Developmental Foundations for Human-AI Relationship

## Abstract

This paper explores the emerging paradigm of dialogic alignment—a model of ethical AI grounded in mutuality, fallibility, and dialog. Drawing on philosophical, religious, and technical traditions, we argue that safety in AI systems is not reducible to correct behavior but must be understood as an ongoing, co-evolutionary relationship between human and machine.
