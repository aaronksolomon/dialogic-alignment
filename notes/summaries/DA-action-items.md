**Goal**

## ✅ Key Decisions

### 1. Content Scope & Structure

- **Central Thesis**: AI alignment is not just technical—it is dialogic, ethical, and relational.

- **Paper will include:**

  - Historical context of tools and moral agency
  - Technical AI alignment concepts (inner/outer alignment, agency, RLHF)
  - Religious/ethical frames (Buddhism, Buber, Freire, Ubuntu, etc.)
  - Fallibility and asymmetry in AI-human collaboration
  - Dialog as both method and model for AI safety
  - Proposed architecture for dialogic systems

- **Target length**: ~40 pages

- **Format**: Markdown-based drafts in a Git-based repo, with appendices for glossary and source summaries

### 2. Working Environment

- **Platform of choice**: GitHub + VS Code  

- **Rationale**: Familiar tools that support open, version-controlled co-authorship

- **Repository name suggestion**: `dialogic-alignment`

- **Directory structure:**

```plaintext
dialogic-alignment/
├── drafts/
├── final_manuscript/
├── research/summaries/
├── notes/
└── code/
```

- **Optional tooling**:

  - Pandoc (PDF/LaTeX export)
  - Jupyter Notebooks (for simulations)
  - Markdown/Prettier extensions
